Elite Neon Cup — Athens 2027

A recruitment pathway for Greek-heritage players at NPL level — selected and managed by Ginga Global Group to compete internationally in Athens, Greece.

About the Program

What is the Elite Neon Cup?

The Elite Neon Cup is an international youth football tournament held in Athens, Greece. For Greek-heritage players in Australia, it's a rare opportunity to represent their heritage on the world stage — competing against international sides while developing their game in an elite environment.

Ginga Global Group selects and manages the Australian squad, handling trials, heritage confirmation, travel logistics and on-the-ground support throughout the tournament. We keep the process direct — players and families deal with us, not intermediaries.

Who can apply
  • Born 2011 or 2013
  • Currently playing NPL-level football
  • Greek heritage (player, parent or grandparent)
  • Based in Sydney, Melbourne or Adelaide

Register below and our team will follow up directly to confirm eligibility and next steps.

The Process

How we build the squad.

From open trials through to confirmed selection, here's what the pathway looks like for players and families.

Trials

Open trial sessions are currently running in Sydney and Melbourne. Coaches evaluate technical ability, game understanding and coachability across a range of drills and small-sided games — every eligible player gets a fair assessment.

Shortlisting

After trials close, our coaching staff review all candidates and narrow the squad to the strongest group for Athens. Shortlisted players and their families are contacted directly — there's no public announcement list.

Confirmation

Selected players confirm their spot by verifying Greek heritage documentation. From there, our team handles all logistics: travel, accommodation, match schedule and on-the-ground support throughout the tournament.
