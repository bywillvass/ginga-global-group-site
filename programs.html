Elite Neon Cup — Athens 2027. A recruitment pathway for Greek-heritage players at NPL level.

Who this is for

The Elite Neon Cup gives eligible players the chance to represent their heritage on an international stage in Athens. We're currently shortlisting for the 2027 squad.

  • Born 2011 or 2013
  • Currently playing NPL-level football
  • Greek heritage (player, parent or grandparent)
  • Based in Sydney, Melbourne or Adelaide

Register below and our team will follow up directly to confirm eligibility and next steps.
