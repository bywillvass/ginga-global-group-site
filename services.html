Player Development

We scout, mentor and prepare players for the next level.

From skill growth to professional exposure, we help players become the kind of prospect clubs can't ignore — with a clear, honest read on where they're at and what's next.

This includes ongoing scouting, one-on-one guidance through NPL-level competition, and preparation for trials and representative selection.

Club & Coach Connections

Linking clubs with talented players and experienced coaches.

We work both sides of the relationship — helping clubs find the players and coaching talent they need, and helping players find a club environment that actually develops them.

These relationships are built on GGG's existing network across NPL and futsal competitions in Sydney, Melbourne and Adelaide.

Elite International Tours

Training camps and tournaments overseas.

We organise elite training camps and international tours that put players in front of a higher standard of competition. Recent tours have taken players to Brazil and Thailand, with more than 500 players and parents travelling with GGG across Spain, Brazil, Thailand and England to date.
