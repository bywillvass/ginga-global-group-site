Brazil Camp 2024

Two weeks in Brazil. Professional stadiums, elite coaches, and matches against some of the country's most iconic clubs.

Destination São Paulo & Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
Year 2024
Duration Two weeks
Program Type International Training Camp
About the Tour

A football journey like no other.

The 2024 Brazil Camp brought GGG players and coaches on an unforgettable adventure through Brazil. The squad trained under professional coaches in professional stadiums, competed against top-tier Brazilian clubs, and experienced football culture at its rawest and most passionate.

More than a trip — it was a milestone. Players returned with a sharper game, a broader perspective, and memories that will last a lifetime. The tour set the foundation for even bigger opportunities ahead in developing Australian talent on the world stage.

Clubs we faced
  • Corinthians
  • Botafogo
  • Aster FC
  • Juventus
  • Itaquaquecetuba FC

Highlights

What we experienced.

Two weeks of football, culture and competition across São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro.

Professional Training

Players trained under elite Brazilian coaches inside professional stadiums — an environment that pushed every member of the squad to a new standard and gave them a real taste of what football looks like at the highest level.

Estádio Olímpico Nilton Santos

The standout moment of the trip — facing Botafogo at Estádio Olímpico Nilton Santos. Running out onto that pitch, in front of a crowd, was the kind of experience that changes a player's perspective on what's possible.

Culture & Connection

Beyond the football, the squad visited Rio's beaches, attended live matches, and explored the streets of São Paulo — fully immersed in a country where football isn't a sport, it's a way of life. Lifelong friendships were forged on this trip.
